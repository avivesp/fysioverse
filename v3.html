Education & Innovation

Learning through play

Why tycoon mechanics?

The game creates a system where finance, capacity, quality, access, wellbeing, reputation and innovation interact. Learners cannot solve everything at once.

Learning cycle

Act → experience consequences → reflect → change strategy → try again.

Learning objectives

Strategic reasoning
Recognise trade-offs and second-order consequences.
Professional decision-making
Balance stakeholders, quality, access, people and money.
Innovation
Evaluate hybrid/digital services against a real purpose.
Reflection
Use performance feedback to adapt your next strategy.
Examination product

Fysioverse

Integrated product

This game is the interactive core of the Education & Innovation product. The final package can embed the self-produced educational video and interactive assessment alongside it.

Technology

Browser-first, static deployment through GitHub/Vercel. Babylon.js provides the 3D clinic layer with a lightweight UI that remains usable if 3D fails.

Privacy & ethics

No account, analytics or real patient data are required. Progress is stored locally. Characters are fictional. Dutch policy is represented as gameplay abstraction and must be checked against current official sources for submission.
