Play Decision Lab

Learning Hub

Use these concepts before or after a game session. The goal is not to memorize isolated facts; it is to connect professional principles to decisions made in a realistic practice context.

How to use it: choose a topic, play a few clinic days, then return to the Decision Lab and explain what you would change. This creates a cycle of learn → apply → reflect → improve.
Quality

Learning and improvement

Think in cycles: identify a problem, choose an intervention, measure what happens, reflect on the result and adapt. Fysioverse makes this visible through quality, reputation, access and team outcomes.

Patient-centred care

Care around the patient

Consider goals, barriers, access and preferences rather than treating the clinic as a production line. Game situations deliberately create tensions between capacity, money and patient needs.

Appropriate care

The right care, right place

Ask whether a problem needs in-person treatment, adaptation, collaboration or a digital/hybrid option. Decisions can improve access while also creating new quality and workload questions.

Professional development

Develop the team

Recruitment, coaching, workload and wellbeing are part of practice quality. Sustainable performance requires attention to people as well as financial results.

Digital physiotherapy

Use technology deliberately

Hybrid care is not automatically better. Consider who benefits, what barriers appear, what quality safeguards are needed and how technology changes the workflow.

Entrepreneurship

Run a sustainable practice

Revenue, costs, contracts, investment and capacity interact with professional responsibilities. A financially strong clinic can still fail if quality, access or team wellbeing collapse.

Collaboration

Work across domains

Physiotherapy does not exist in isolation. Consider referral networks, community partners and multidisciplinary collaboration when a patient need exceeds the clinic's own capacity.

Reflection

Explain your reasoning

The most useful learning question is not simply “what happened?” but “why did I choose this, what happened because of it, and what would I do differently next time?”
