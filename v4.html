Education & Innovation

Learning through play

Why this game has depth

The simulation makes quality, access, finance, workforce wellbeing, reputation and innovation compete for attention. Learners can pursue different viable strategies rather than one correct path.

Loop

Plan → act → observe → deal with uncertainty → reflect → adapt → repeat.

Objectives

Strategic reasoning
Recognise trade-offs and second-order effects.
Professional decision-making
Balance stakeholders, people, quality, access and money.
Innovation
Evaluate digital/hybrid services by purpose and consequences.
Reflection
Use feedback and performance data to change strategy.
Examination product

Fysioverse

Integrated E&I product

The game is the interactive core. A self-produced educational video and personalized assessment can sit alongside it for the final examination package.

Technical design

Browser-first and deployable through GitHub/Vercel. Babylon.js provides the interactive 3D layer; gameplay rules remain deterministic and client-side.

Ethics & privacy

No account, analytics or real patient data are required. Characters are fictional. Policy and reimbursement are represented as gameplay abstractions and should be checked against current official sources before submission.
