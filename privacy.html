Privacy & responsible use

This page describes the current prototype/examination implementation. It is not legal advice. The implementation is deliberately designed to avoid collecting personal or patient-identifiable data.

No account required

Fysioverse currently does not require a student account, login, name, email address or patient record. The Decision Lab calculates its profile in the browser and does not submit the answers to a server.

Game saves

The current clinic simulation stores game progress in the browser's localStorage so a learner can continue the simulation. Clearing browser storage can remove the local save. No real patient data should ever be entered.

Fictional cases

All patients, staff and practice situations in the simulation are fictional. The product is an educational simulation and must not be used as a real patient record, clinical decision-support system or substitute for professional judgement.

Third-party technology

The 3D game currently loads Babylon.js from the Babylon.js CDN. The production implementation should keep third-party dependencies documented and reviewed before final submission. The examination version should also document any video host, analytics, fonts or other external services that are added later.

Cookies and consent

The current implementation does not intentionally deploy an analytics or advertising cookie system. If analytics, embedded video services or other tracking technologies are added, the final examination product must document what is used and implement the required consent/information flow where applicable.

Security and continuity

The app is deployed through GitHub and Vercel. Production changes are versioned in Git. The educational content is static and does not depend on a student database. This reduces the amount of personal data and infrastructure that needs protection.
