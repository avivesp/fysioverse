Fysioverse is an experiential learning simulation for students and early-career physiotherapists. You run a fictional Dutch physiotherapy practice and experience how clinical quality, patient-centred care, access, people, entrepreneurship and innovation interact.

Play

Build and manage a living physiotherapy practice. Patients arrive, staff work, rooms matter and decisions create consequences.

🧠

Learn

Connect gameplay to quality, appropriate care, professional development, accessibility, digital care and entrepreneurship.

🧭

Reflect

Complete scenario-based decisions and receive an individual profile with automated feedback explaining your choices.

Learning before, during and after play

The simulation is the experiential core. The Learning Hub provides the conceptual layer, while the Decision Lab makes the learner's reasoning visible and gives personalized feedback. Together they create a coherent learning journey rather than a game with information attached.
