What this project covers

Fysioverse is structured to make the educational, interactive, technical, legal and ethical components visible as one coherent online product.

🎓 Educational purposeExperiential learning through decisions, consequences, reflection, feedback and repeated practice.
🎯 Target audiencePhysiotherapy students and early-career physiotherapists learning to combine professional responsibility with practice management.
🏥 Clinical/professional contextFictional Dutch physiotherapy practice with quality, access, patient needs, team wellbeing, collaboration and appropriate-care choices.
💶 EntrepreneurshipCash flow, capacity, staffing, investment, contracts, reputation and sustainable growth create competing priorities.
💻 Digital & innovationHybrid care, service design and innovation choices are treated as decisions that require quality and accessibility safeguards.
🧭 Personalized assessmentThe Decision Lab uses user input to generate automated feedback without requiring names, accounts or identifiable patient information.
♿ Accessibility & responsive designReadable interface, clear navigation, meaningful labels, responsive layouts and keyboard-friendly controls are built into the product.
🔐 Privacy & ethicsNo student account is required; gameplay uses fictional patients and local browser storage rather than a student database.
© Copyright & attributionExternal libraries, services and other resources are disclosed through the Credits & AI page.
🧪 Testing & continuityThe product is designed for browser-based access, version-controlled updates and repeatable Vercel deployment from GitHub.

Educational media & assessment

Integrated video component: the examination product is designed to host the required self-produced educational video directly inside this environment. The final student-produced video must be embedded before submission; it cannot be fabricated by the application. The video should explain a clear educational/clinical purpose and meet the course requirements for format, accessibility, consent and copyright.

Responsible by design

Data minimisation is intentional.
No account, name, email address, student number or identifiable patient information is required. Game progress and Decision Lab feedback can remain local to the learner's browser. The simulation uses fictional patients. Third-party services and AI use are documented separately so the learner can understand what is happening.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need an account?

No. The core experience is designed to work without student accounts or identifying data.

Are the patients real?

No. All patient situations in the simulation are fictional educational scenarios.

Is this a clinical decision tool?

No. Fysioverse is an educational simulation. It is not a substitute for clinical guidelines, professional judgement or real patient assessment.

What should I do first?

Play a few days in the Tycoon, then use the Learning Hub and Decision Lab to connect your choices to professional reasoning.
