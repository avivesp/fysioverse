Fysioverse is an experiential learning simulation for students and early-career physiotherapists. Build a fictional Dutch physiotherapy practice and experience how clinical quality, patient-centred care, access, people, entrepreneurship and innovation interact.

🏥

PLAY

Build rooms, hire staff, grow demand, manage cash, negotiate contracts and respond to practice situations. Your choices have consequences.

🧠

LEARN

Connect gameplay to quality improvement, patient-centred care, appropriate care, professional development, digital care and entrepreneurship.

🧭

DECIDE

Complete scenario-based decisions and receive personalized feedback on your reasoning profile without creating an account.

Why it feels like a tycoon

The practice is no longer just a visual scene. Capacity, patient demand, cash, reputation, access, team wellbeing and innovation interact over days, weeks and years. Rooms visibly change the clinic, objectives give progression, and events create trade-offs rather than single correct answers.

Responsible by design

No account is required. Game progress and Decision Lab feedback are processed locally in the browser. The simulation uses fictional patients and does not ask learners for names, emails, student numbers or patient information. See Privacy and Credits & AI.
