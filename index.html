Education & Innovation examination product

Build a physiotherapy practice that actually survives.

Fysioverse is a serious game for graduate physiotherapists. You balance clinical quality, patient access, staff wellbeing, finance and strategic choices — then receive personalised feedback on how you managed the practice.

Learning goals

By playing, you practise:

1 · Strategic reasoningRecognise trade-offs rather than optimising one metric.
2 · Professional decision-makingExplain choices involving quality, access, people and money.
3 · ReflectionUse your year report and assessment feedback to identify strengths and risks.
4 · Innovation thinkingEvaluate hybrid care and service improvements without treating technology as an end in itself.

Learning design

Why Fysioverse is educational

Learning by doing

The simulation creates consequences for decisions so learners can experience systems thinking rather than only recall information.

Reflection

The year report makes the learner interpret performance and identify neglected dimensions.

Feedback

The assessment maps choices to actionable feedback. The game report also highlights the player's strongest and weakest management dimensions.

Didactic principles

Active learningThe learner acts inside a problem environment.
Experiential learningExperience → consequence → reflection → next attempt.
Authentic contextThe context is a Dutch physiotherapy practice, while gameplay abstractions are clearly labelled.
Formative assessmentFeedback supports improvement rather than ranking learners.

Examination justification

Product rationale

Aim & audience

Fysioverse supports graduate physiotherapists in practising the complex reasoning involved in building and running a physiotherapy practice.

Need & context

Practice ownership combines clinical, organisational, financial and professional responsibilities. A simulation allows these dimensions to be experienced together.

Innovation

The innovation is not “3D for 3D's sake”: the environment visualises a living practice while the simulation engine creates meaningful trade-offs and reflection.

Technical choices

Fysioverse is a lightweight web application deployable through GitHub and Vercel. Core simulation state is deterministic enough to support repeatable learning, while event variation adds replay value.

Design & accessibility

Responsive layout, semantic headings, high-contrast UI, text alternatives for game information, keyboard-friendly controls and no essential information conveyed by colour alone are design goals.

AI

The current core game rules do not depend on generative AI. This keeps educational outcomes reproducible and auditable. AI can be added later as an optional reflection coach.

Legal & ethical approach

No patient-identifiable information is collected. The game uses fictional simulated patients. No account is required. No analytics or advertising are required by the core experience. Dutch reimbursement, contracting and professional information should be verified against current official sources before submission.

Privacy & security

Privacy by design

✓ No account requiredThe prototype works without registration.
✓ No real patient dataPatients are fictional simulation entities only.
✓ Local save onlyGame progress is stored in the browser's localStorage; it is not a patient record or cloud database.
✓ Minimal data collectionThe assessment is processed in the browser and does not require identifying information.
Cookies / analyticsNo analytics or advertising cookies are required for the core prototype.
