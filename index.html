Everything in the game is fictional and educational. No real patient data is used.

Education & Innovation

Learning through simulation

Why a tycoon game?

Running a practice is a systems problem. Financial decisions affect capacity. Capacity affects wellbeing. Wellbeing affects quality. Quality affects reputation and demand. The game lets the learner experience these relationships.

Reflection loop

At the end of every year, the game identifies the player's strongest and weakest dimensions and turns the result into a next-step reflection.

Learning objectives

Strategic reasoningBalance competing dimensions instead of maximising one.
Professional decision-makingConsider stakeholders, consequences, risks and evidence.
InnovationTest digital/hybrid services as means to a purpose.
ReflectionUse feedback to change the next strategy.
Examination product

Fysioverse — what is included

Interactive product

This game is the central interactive environment. It includes story, simulation, personalised feedback, save/load and a multi-year progression system.

Video

The final examination version will embed the self-produced 16:9 educational video here. The course requirements for active contribution, appearance/speaking and resolution remain part of the production checklist.

Privacy & ethics

No account is required. Progress is stored locally in the browser. Characters are fictional. The game should not be used to store or process real patient information.

Technical

The 3D world uses Babylon.js in the browser, with a lightweight fallback UI. The project is static and deployable through GitHub/Vercel.
