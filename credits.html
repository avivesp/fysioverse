Credits, sources & AI transparency

Educational basis

The examination concept is aligned with the uploaded Education & Innovation course manual. The physiotherapy quality framing is informed by KNGF's Quality Framework for Physiotherapy and Exercise Therapy, including appropriate care, patient-centred principles, learning and improvement, and transparency of quality.

Technology

The 3D clinic uses Babylon.js. The web product is version-controlled in GitHub and deployed through Vercel. These dependencies are documented so the final justification can explain platform choice, continuity and deployment.

AI use

AI assistance was used during development for ideation, educational structuring, interface/code generation, debugging and iterative refinement. AI-generated material is treated as draft output: factual educational claims need human review against authoritative sources before final submission. AI is not used to generate real patient records or to make clinical decisions for a real patient.

Copyright

The current game artwork is procedurally generated in the browser rather than copied from external image libraries. Babylon.js is loaded as a third-party software dependency. Before final submission, the project team should retain a source/licence list for every external asset, font, icon, video and library used.

Important examination note

This page is part of the product, but the written justification remains the authoritative place to explain the full educational rationale, didactic model, UX choices, testing results, AI use, privacy, copyright, security and continuity plan.
