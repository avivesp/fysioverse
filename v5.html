How KNGF-informed play works

The game turns professional-quality concepts into situations rather than flashcards. Choices are scored against patient-centred care, quality, access, professional development and sustainability. Official KNGF guidance should be checked against the current version before academic submission.

Product

Fysioverse

A serious game about becoming a physiotherapy entrepreneur

Fysioverse combines a living 3D clinic, management simulation, professional scenarios, personalised feedback and reflective learning. It deliberately uses fictional patients and gameplay abstractions.

Privacy by design

No real patient data, no required account and no analytics are needed for the core game. Saves remain in the browser.
