KNGF-informed, not KNGF-branded advice

The current KNGF Quality Framework describes what patients may expect and how providers work on quality, including evaluating, improving, monitoring and making quality visible. Fysioverse turns those ideas into gameplay situations rather than memorisation.

For an examination submission, the final justification should cite the exact KNGF documents used and distinguish game abstractions from professional guidance.
